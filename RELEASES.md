#### What's new

- Pair your phone with **Our Sword** by scanning a QR code.
- Control the app over your local Wi-Fi network.
- Open pairing links directly with `oursword-remote://pair...` URLs.
- Clear pairing flow with camera permission requested only when needed.

#### Installation

1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded file and allow installs from your browser or file manager when prompted.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **Our Sword Remote**.

> Google Play Protect may warn about apps installed outside the Play Store. Tap **More details → Install anyway** to continue.

#### Requirements

- Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer
- Wi-Fi connection on the same local network as the target device
- Camera (for scanning the pairing QR code)
